# Mission

## Vision

RMS is a **full shared-living operating system** — a single place where roommates, flatmates, and co-living groups manage every practical aspect of living together: finances, chores, supplies, house rules, and communication.

## Problem

Shared living runs on trust, but trust breaks down when records are informal. Today's groups rely on WhatsApp polls, mental ledgers, and awkward "you owe me" messages. The result is avoidable conflict, lost money, and uneven workloads — especially in price-sensitive markets where even small amounts matter.

## Who We Serve

**Primary audience:** The South-Asian / Indian shared-flat market — PG residents, college flatmates, and early-career roommates splitting rent and expenses in shared apartments. This market has a strong cultural norm of communal living, but almost no purpose-built tooling for it.

**Design implications of this audience:**

- **Mobile-first** — most users will access RMS from a phone.
- **Low friction** — the tool must be simpler than a group chat, not more complex.
- **Multilingual-ready** — UI copy should stay short and translatable; avoid idioms.
- **Cost-aware** — the product must remain free or very low-cost to match the audience's budget sensitivity.

## Core Principles

1. **Single source of truth** — if it's about the flat, it's in RMS. No side channels needed.
2. **Fairness by default** — splits, rotations, and assignments should be transparent and auditable so no one feels cheated.
3. **Minimal overhead** — adding an expense or logging a chore should take fewer taps than sending a text message.
4. **Privacy-respecting** — financial data stays between the people in the room. No ads, no data selling.

## Scope Pillars

| Pillar | What it covers | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| **Finances** | Shared expenses, debt tracking, partial payments, settlement | Backend complete; frontend in progress |
| **Chores** | Task assignment, rotation schedules, completion tracking | Planned (landing-page copy only) |
| **Supplies** | Shared shopping lists, restock reminders | Not started |
| **House Rules** | Shared agreements, quiet hours, guest policies | Not started |
| **Communication** | Announcements, polls, in-app notifications | Not started |

> [!NOTE]
> The first two pillars (Finances + Chores) define the MVP. Supplies, House Rules, and Communication are future expansions that keep the "operating system" vision alive without overloading the initial build.
